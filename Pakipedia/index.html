Pakistan

From Pakipedia, the free encyclopedia

Pakistan, officially the Islamic Republic of Pakistan, is a country in South Asia. It is the world's fifth-most populous country, with a population exceeding 240 million people, and has the world's second-largest Muslim population. Pakistan is the 33rd-largest country by area, spanning 881,913 square kilometers (340,509 square miles).

Global & Regional Presence

Pakistan holds a strategically vital location at the intersection of South Asia, Central Asia, and the Middle East. It has a coastline along the Arabian Sea and the Gulf of Oman in the south, and shares land borders with India to the east, Afghanistan to the west, Iran to the southwest, and China to the northeast.

  • Geopolitical Importance: Home to the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC), a major hub for regional trade and connectivity.
  • International Roles: Active member of the United Nations, Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO), Commonwealth of Nations, and Organization of Islamic Cooperation (OIC).
  • Defense Capability: Pakistan is a declared nuclear-weapons state and possesses the sixth-largest standing armed forces in the world.

Historical Background

The territory that comprises modern Pakistan is a cradle of ancient civilizations and served as a historical crossroad for various empires.

  • Indus Valley Civilization: One of the world's oldest urban civilizations flourished around 2500 BCE in sites like Mohenjo-daro and Harappa.
  • Gandhara Kingdom: A major center of Buddhist culture, art, and learning centered around Taxila from the 6th century BCE.
  • Islamic Empires: Region dominated by various dynasties, including the Umayyad conquest under Muhammad bin Qasim, the Delhi Sultanate, and later the Mughal Empire.
  • Independence (1947): Emerging from British India following the Pakistan Movement led by Muhammad Ali Jinnah, Pakistan became an independent sovereign state on August 14, 1947.
