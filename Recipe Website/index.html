Recipe

Preparation Time: 5 minutes

Cooking Time: 30–40 minutes

Step-by-step Instructions

1- Prepare the Tea Concentrate (Kehwa)

In a deep saucepan, combine 2 cups of cold water, 2 tbsp Kashmiri green tea leaves, 3–4
crushed cardamom pods, 1 star anise, 1 small cinnamon stick, and 1/4 tsp baking soda.

2- Shock the Tea (The Secret to the Pink Color)

Pour 1 cup of ice-cold water (along with 1/2 cup of ice cubes) directly into the boiling reduction.

Aerate the tea by using a ladle to scoop up the liquid and pour it back into the pot from a height continuously for 3–5 minutes. This process introduces oxygen, causing the baking soda and tannins to react and set the bright pink hue.

3- Add Milk & Sweetener

Reduce the heat to low and pour in 2 cups of whole milk. Watch the mixture instantly transform into a signature pastel pink color.z

Add sugar or salt to taste (traditional Kashmiri style uses salt, while commercial versions prefer sugar).

Let the tea simmer gently on low heat for 3–5 minutes until heated through and creamy.

Garnish & Serve

Pour the hot Kashmiri chai into warm cups or clay mugs.

Top generously with finely crushed pistachios, sliced almonds, and a few dried rose petals. Serve hot alongside traditional flatbread or biscuits.
